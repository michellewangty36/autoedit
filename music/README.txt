Put your own songs here (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, flac) and AutoEdit will use them.

Put a song in the folder for its mood so the right style picks it:
  upbeat/     vlogs, reels, energetic
  happy/      travel, fun
  chill/      relaxed, lo-fi, aesthetic
  cinematic/  epic, dramatic, movie trailers
  emotional/  memories, family, weddings
Songs placed directly in this folder (not in a mood folder) can be used for any style.

If this folder is empty, AutoEdit composes a brand-new royalty-free track for every video.

Free music you are allowed to use in videos:
  - YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com > Audio Library)
  - Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music)
  - Free Music Archive (freemusicarchive.org) - check each song's licence
Popular chart songs are copyrighted: YouTube/TikTok/Instagram may mute or block videos that use them.
